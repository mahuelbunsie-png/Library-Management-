# Library Director - Individual Requirements

**Group:** 27  
**Project:** Library Management  
**Stakeholder:** Library Director  
**Role Type:** Primary Role

## Individual Requirements

### Functional Requirements

**DIR-01:** The system shall provide the Library Director with the number of
books currently checked out.

**DIR-02:** The system shall provide the Library Director with information
identifying the most popular books.

**DIR-03:** The system shall provide the Library Director with the number of
active library members.

**DIR-04:** The system shall provide the Library Director with reports about
library activity.

**DIR-05:** The system shall provide measurable information that can be used
to evaluate changes in late book returns.

### Non-Functional Requirements

**DIR-06:** The system shall be affordable for the library to operate and
maintain within its limited budget.

**DIR-07:** The system shall not require on-site IT staff for routine
operation and maintenance.

**DIR-08:** The system shall be designed so that additional capabilities,
such as e-books or inter-library loans, can be added in the future without
requiring a complete redesign of the system.

## Unresolved Policy Requirement

**DIR-09:** The system shall support an overdue-book policy that balances
encouraging timely returns with avoiding overly punitive consequences for
library members.

## Unresolved and Unspecified Details

Several requirements contain details that were not fully specified during
elicitation. These details have intentionally not been invented.

### DIR-02 - Most Popular Books

The Director wants to know which books are the most popular, but "most
popular" is not defined. For example, it is unclear whether popularity should
be measured by total checkouts, holds, or another measure.

### DIR-03 - Active Members

The Director wants to know how many library members are active, but the
meaning of an "active" member was not specified.

### DIR-04 - Library Activity Reports

The Director wants useful reports about library activity. The required
reporting information includes the number of books currently checked out,
popular books, and active members, but the frequency, format, and any
additional information required in these reports were not specified.

### DIR-05 - Measurable Improvement

The Director needs concrete information to justify the project to city
council and wants to determine whether the system reduces late returns.
However, no target percentage or measurement period was specified.

### DIR-06 - Affordability

The library has a very limited budget and cannot afford an expensive
commercial system. However, an exact maximum operating or maintenance budget
was not specified.

### DIR-07 - Maintenance

The library does not have IT staff on-site, so the system should not be
difficult or expensive to maintain. The exact level of maintenance that is
acceptable was not specified.

### DIR-08 - Future Extensibility

The Director wants the system to support future expansion, including possible
e-book and inter-library loan functionality. These capabilities are future
possibilities and are not requirements for the current system.

### DIR-09 - Overdue-Book Policy

The Director has not made a final decision about how overdue books should be
handled.

The Director is concerned about late returns because they affect the
library's ability to keep books circulating. At the same time, the Director
does not want the library to adopt a policy that may unfairly affect
lower-income patrons.

The Director is leaning toward a small grace period followed by a modest fine
for repeated lateness, but this is not a final policy decision.

Therefore, the overdue-book policy remains unresolved and should not be
converted into a specific fine amount or grace-period length without a final
decision.

## Identified Stakeholder Conflict

The Director's position on overdue books exists between two conflicting
stakeholder positions.

The Librarian supports a small daily fine for overdue books because late
returns prevent other members from accessing books.

The Library Member opposes late fines and prefers reminders or a grace period.

The Director recognizes both concerns and has not made a final decision. The
Director is leaning toward a grace period followed by a modest fine for
repeated lateness but does not consider this a final decision.

Therefore, the overdue-book policy remains an open stakeholder conflict and
should be addressed during requirements consolidation rather than resolved
by selecting one stakeholder's position.
