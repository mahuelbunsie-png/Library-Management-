# Librarian (Front-Desk Staff) - Role-Play Prework

**Stakeholder:** Librarian / Front-Desk Staff  
**Group:** 27  
**Project:** Library Management  
**Role Type:** Primary Role  
**Prepared by:** Mahuel Bunsie

## Role Overview

For the requirements elicitation session, I will represent the Librarian / 
Front-Desk Staff stakeholder. My responses during the interview will be based 
on the needs, concerns, and priorities described in my assigned stakeholder 
role card.

The purpose of the interview is to identify requirements from the Librarian's 
perspective without assuming system features before they have been elicited.

## Stakeholder Priorities

Based on the Librarian role, the main areas I will keep in mind during the 
role-play are:

- Completing common front-desk tasks efficiently.
- Finding books and library members quickly.
- Having accurate information about whether books are available or checked out.
- Identifying overdue books.
- Addressing the problem of overdue book returns.
- Having consequences for overdue books.
- Keeping common front-desk interactions simple and easy to complete.

## Current Concerns

The Librarian's concerns with the current process include:

- Book information may not always accurately reflect whether a book is currently
  available or checked out.
- Finding the required information can take too long when members are waiting
  at the front desk.
- Overdue books create problems when other members are waiting for them.
- Common library transactions should not require unnecessary or complicated
  interactions with the system.

## Requirements Elicitation Approach

During the meeting, my group member will interview me while I remain in the
Librarian role.

The interview should use open-ended questions to discover the Librarian's
requirements rather than assuming specific system features in advance.

Example areas for discussion include:

1. What tasks does the Librarian perform at the front desk?
2. What problems exist with the current process?
3. How does the Librarian find books and members?
4. What information does the Librarian need when helping a member?
5. What information is important when checking a book in or out?
6. What problems are caused by overdue books?
7. How should overdue books be handled?
8. What makes a front-desk transaction difficult or inefficient?
9. What would make the new system easier for the Librarian to use?
10. What would make the new system successful from the Librarian's perspective?

## Notes for Role-Play

- Stay within the Librarian stakeholder perspective.
- Do not invent requirements that are not supported by the role.
- Do not attempt to resolve disagreements with other stakeholders during the
  individual interview.
- Record unclear or incomplete needs rather than making assumptions.
- Clarify requirements through follow-up questions when appropriate.
- Requirements will be written as "The system shall..." statements after the
  elicitation interview.

## Post-Interview

After the interview, the information elicited from the Librarian will be
converted into an individual Librarian requirements list.

The Librarian requirements will later be combined with:

- The Library Member's individual requirements.
- The jointly elicited Library Director requirements.

The three lists will then be consolidated and evaluated for conflict,
ambiguity, completeness, consistency, testability, and evolvability.
